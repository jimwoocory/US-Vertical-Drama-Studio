# US writing review reference

Use the following only with cited project material.

| Review claim | Evidence threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Craft score | Scene/line/action evidence for every scorecard item. The 100-point model and 85 threshold are internal craft heuristics, not commercial calibration. |
| Due promise | Approved Contract/Bible/Ledger source, deadline, and payoff evidence; a change requires approved rationale, replacement delivery, and new deadline. |
| Cross-episode / arc / season result | Relevant comparison material. Otherwise mark `NOT ASSESSED`; do not present the scope as passed. |
| Audience / commercial validation | Actual study, feedback, release, campaign, or transaction evidence with method, result, scope, and source. Internal simulation is `UNTESTED`. |

Contractual free, subscription, and coin-content boundaries may constrain content. Pricing, ad technical failures, and refunds are not screenplay-quality evidence.

Compact scorecard example: `Reversal 11/15 | S03 L18–26: offer changes Maya's leverage | consequence underplayed | rewrite the cost on screen`.
