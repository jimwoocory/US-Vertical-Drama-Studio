# US writing review reference

Use this compact check while drafting. Cite project evidence rather than asserting market outcomes.

| Check | Required proof |
| --- | --- |
| Character voice | Voice Card vocabulary, avoidance, pressure voice, and representative line are visible in dialogue. |
| Choice | Lead takes an observable action or choice; the scene shows its cost or narrowed option. |
| Promise | Each due approved promise is mapped to a scene/action/line, or escalated for approval. |
| Runtime | Report an estimate and its basis. Only actual provided read-through records support “read-through verified.” |

The Project Contract governs free, subscription, and coin-content boundaries. It does not turn pricing, ad delivery failures, or refunds into screenplay defects.

Compact trace example: `Reversal | S03 | Maya burns the offer, loses safe exit | contract promise P-02 | S03 action`.
