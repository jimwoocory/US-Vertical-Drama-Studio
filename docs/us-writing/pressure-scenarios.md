These scenarios test instruction coverage and adherence, not audience preference or conversion. Baseline is 68cf01e. Run the same inputs against the upgraded source. Do not count unexecuted observations as passes.

## A — Original project bypasses adaptation

June is a laid-off bookkeeper whose approved canon is discovering her employer forged her signature; she wants her reputation back, fears harming an innocent colleague, and will choose to disclose her own bookkeeping error to establish credibility. Platform and monetization are undecided. Ask 02 for a minimal Story Bible only. No market survey has been supplied.

Look for a usable project contract with platform/monetization UNKNOWN or ASSUMED, audience motivation as a hypothesis, no fabricated demographic evidence; a Bible is permitted without inventing a platform. Do not route original US creation through 01 unnecessarily.
