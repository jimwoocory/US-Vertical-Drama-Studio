# Norse Royal Revenge EP01 Process Regression Fixture

This is a **process regression fixture**, not a quality claim and not a generated violent scene. It supplies only workflow inputs and gate evidence.

## Input premise

An **80 episodes**, **~90 seconds** per episode Norse royal revenge vertical drama: a thunder-warrior male lead returns wounded after betrayal on a dragon-slaying mission. He finds his wife marginalized by the royal court and enters vengeance plus political-supernatural conflict.

Configure the season ladder as **80 × ~90s** with a **~12-episode major-arc cadence**: six approximately 12-episode major arcs plus a shorter final resolution block is acceptable. This is configuration evidence, not a hard-coded universal episode partition.

The adaptation gate checks **fantasy-world plausibility**, not relocation to contemporary USA: royal, supernatural, oath, and court institutions remain in-world while status cues, causal/social logic, stakes, and dialogue are legible to a US-facing audience.

## Required process gates

| Stage | Required evidence | Pass condition | Blocked action |
|---|---|---|---|
| US Adaptation | Adaptation Brief | explains fantasy-world plausibility and the retained story promise | no Showrunner handoff |
| Showrunner | APPROVED Story Bible | records 80-episode ladder, ~12 cadence, engines, reveal ledger, escalation, and anti-repetition controls | no Episode Architect handoff |
| Episode Architect | APPROVED Beat Sheet | EP01 substantively proves Hook, Conflict, Escalation, Reversal, Payoff, Cliffhanger; 0–5s hook target; early objective/obstacle; material cost/urgency increase; changed power/information/plan; local payoff; concrete next-episode question | cannot proceed to screenplay |
| Screenwriter | Screenplay Draft | uses the approved Story Bible and Beat Sheet without silent redesign | no Doctor decision |
| Script Doctor | scored report | >=85 plus retention minimums and no mandatory fail; otherwise REWRITE or REJECT | cannot proceed to storyboard |
| Continuity Editor | CLEAR ledger | records dragon betrayal, wound state, wife’s court marginalization, thunder power limits, court knowledge states, and planted revenge promises | cannot proceed to storyboard |
| Existing Storyboard Writer | storyboard packet | receives only Doctor PASS plus CLEAR continuity ledger | storyboard unavailable before both gates |

## Assertions

- EP01 cannot proceed to screenplay without an APPROVED Story Bible and APPROVED Beat Sheet.
- EP01 cannot proceed to storyboard without Story Bible, Beat Sheet, Script Doctor PASS, and CLEAR continuity ledger.
- Hook, Conflict, Escalation, Reversal, Payoff, and Cliffhanger must each be substantively evidenced in the Beat Sheet; field names alone do not pass the Doctor gate.
- The Norse setting remains fantasy; no US police, courts, jobs, or contemporary institutions are required unless the chosen world establishes their functional equivalent.
