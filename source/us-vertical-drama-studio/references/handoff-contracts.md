# Handoff Contracts

1. US Adaptation → Showrunner: Adaptation Brief; not canon until incorporated.
2. Showrunner → Episode Architect: APPROVED Story Bible plus ladder, reveal ledger, and repetition controls.
3. Episode Architect → Screenwriter: APPROVED Beat Sheet with Hook, Conflict, Escalation, Reversal, Payoff, Cliffhanger, and continuity delta.
4. Screenwriter → Script Doctor: draft plus unchanged approved packet and beat-to-scene trace.
5. Script Doctor → Continuity Editor: PASS only. REWRITE returns to Screenwriter; REJECT returns to Showrunner/Episode Architect.
6. Continuity Editor → existing Storyboard Writer: CLEAR plus Script Doctor PASS, screenplay, full current approved continuity ledger, Story Bible/reveal constraint extract, power/knowledge baseline, and episode delta.

EP01 cannot proceed to screenplay or storyboard without an APPROVED Story Bible and APPROVED Beat Sheet. A non-PASS script must not hand off to storyboard.
